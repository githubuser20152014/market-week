Do not include stock recommendations, entry/exit price levels, or directional trade calls (e.g. "Long $GLD at $4,581", "Entry confirm: GLD holds $4,550", "Kill switch: ...") in LinkedIn or X posts.

**Why:** User explicitly prohibited this. Social posts are public-facing and making specific trade recommendations on social media creates liability and is not the intended purpose of those channels.

**How to apply:** LinkedIn and X posts should cover the macro narrative and market tensions, then direct readers to Substack for the full trade thesis. Never include tickers with directional calls, price levels, or confirm/kill switch language in social posts.
