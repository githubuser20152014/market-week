---

Always generate the post title from the article content — never use the headline as-is.

**Why:** The headline inside the article is a section header; the post title needs to be engaging, specific, and entice readers to click through on the website, Substack, and email.

**How to apply:** After the user approves the content and before publishing, generate 4-5 title options that:
- Lead with the week's most dramatic number or event
- Hint at the surprising finding or actionable insight without giving it away
- Speak directly to the reader ("your portfolio", "the one trade")
- Ask the user to pick one before proceeding to publish
