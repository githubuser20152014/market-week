**Rules:**
- Lead with the market's contradiction or absurdity — not a neutral summary ("Markets yesterday did the classic move: gunned the engine on peace-deal hopes, then discovered both sides were still literally shooting at each other")
- After stating a number, add a sarcastic or darkly comic observation ("because small-caps are the canaries of domestic credit, and right now they are canaries with a cough")
- Dark humor on geopolitical and earnings catalysts — treat them as darkly comic, not tragic ("Nothing says 'relationship repair' like an Independence Day ultimatum with economic pain as the punchline")
- Short sentences. Never more than two clauses. Sarcasm over hedging.
- State opinions as facts, not possibilities
- Name the trades directly — "Stay away from IGV", "Hold GLD - don't you dare sell it"
- Frame contrarian angles as "the trade nobody is talking about"
- Use vivid labels: "kill switch" not "risk", "regime detector" not "directional tell"
- Cut all hedge language
- One punchy thesis per section — don't bury the lede
- All numbers and tickers stay accurate — tone changes delivery, not data
