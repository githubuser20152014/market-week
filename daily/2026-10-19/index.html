Consider trimming speculative AI infrastructure exposure (ARKW, ROBO) and holding or adding gold (GLD, SGOL) and short-duration Treasuries (SGOV, BIL). Defense (XAR, ITA) gets fresh support from JPMorgan's $5 billion defense push and Putin's summit proposal that conveniently leaves out Kyiv.

Watch S&P futures at 7,749 as the line between a real recovery and a bounce that gets sold by lunch.
