## The Brief

Houthis hit two Saudi airports, Trump hinted he might join the fight, and gold sold off. Oil rallied on a war and the safe haven fell: the dollar took the flight-to-safety bid and gold got the bill. Futures are flat because everyone is waiting on JPMorgan to explain life at a 5.25% yield.

Yesterday was green, and nobody believed it. The Dow added 0.83% to **51,654**, the S&P 500 gained 0.59% to **7,811**, and the Nasdaq rose 0.64% to 27,366. The Russell 2000 managed +0.46%, because small caps borrow at these rates and it shows. The **10-year yield rose 1.3 bps to 5.244%**, so bonds still aren't buying the rally. WTI jumped 1.09% to **$92.85**. Gold dropped 0.44% to **$4,198** during a war. Read that twice.

Houthi drones and missiles hit **King Khalid International in Riyadh** and King Fahd International in Dammam over the weekend. WTI was already parked near $90-91 on **Strait of Hormuz** maritime warnings, so this was less a shock than a second helping. Brent is above $105 and the global supply chain has no inventory cushion left. North Korea fired ballistic missiles too: KOSPI fell 3.65%, and everyone else shrugged.

Watch **7,850 on the S&P** as support: if it holds through an earnings miss, that's constructive.
