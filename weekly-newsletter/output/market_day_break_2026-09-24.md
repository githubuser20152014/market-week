*Daily Edition · Market intelligence at the open*

## The Brief

The 10-year hit 5.11% and stocks acted surprised. The economy came in too strong, so the Fed may hike again, so everything you own got repriced at once. Good news is now the bad news, and the bond market is the only one holding the pen.

The S&P 500 closed at **7,706**, down 0.75%. The Nasdaq lost 1.13% to 26,936. The Russell 2000 took the beating at -1.77%, closing at 2,838, because small caps borrow at floating rates and floating rates just went to the gym. The **10-year surged 14.7 basis points to 5.113%**, the highest since 2007. Gold fell 1.33% to **$4,318**, the dollar index climbed to 101.10, and WTI rose 1.81% to $92.16. That makes it stocks down, gold down, dollar up, oil up. Nobody was safe. Nobody was even hiding well.

The trigger was a flash composite PMI of **58.4**, the fastest private-sector growth since July 2021, plus New York Fed President Williams calling another hike by year-end "reasonable." Odds of an October hike jumped to roughly 77% from about 53% the day before. At a 5% risk-free yield, paying 22x forward earnings needs a story. The story is now on probation. **Bonds never bought the rally.** Equities are just catching up to the news.

## What it means for you

Duration is a bad word today. [TLT](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/TLT) and [EDV](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/EDV) are standing in the road with the yield curve coming at them. **A 5% Treasury is real competition** for speculative tech, unprofitable growth and REITs ([VNQ](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/VNQ)). Cash-like ETFs ([SGOV](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SGOV), [BIL](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/BIL)) now pay you to do nothing. Boring finally has a coupon. Energy ([XLE](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XLE)) is the lone adult in the room: WTI is near **$93.5** this morning on **Strait of Hormuz** risk and a diesel export ban that big business is begging Trump to drop. The Trump-Xi summit is a mild tailwind for chips ([SOXX](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SOXX), [SMH](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SMH)). **Yield pressure is winning that argument** for now.

Futures point lower again: S&P down about 0.5%, Nasdaq down about 0.9%. Asia was a split decision. Nikkei gained 1.30%, Hang Seng lost 0.29%, ASX fell 0.72% and Nifty 50 dropped 1.64%. Europe is slightly lower. KOSPI was closed for Chuseok, so it dodged the whole mess. The next inflation print is the live grenade. **A hot number pushes the 10-year further past 5.1%**, and the equity market gets to relearn what a discount rate is.
