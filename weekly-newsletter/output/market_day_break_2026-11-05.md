## The Brief

Democrats took Congress. Wall Street threw a parade. The **Dow gained 1,570 points (+3.0%) to 53,356**, which is what happens when investors decide gridlock is a feature. The **S&P 500 rose 1.6% to 7,689**, the Nasdaq added 1.4% to 25,737, and the Russell 2000 matched the S&P at +1.6%. Even the small caps came to the party.

**WTI crude cratered 8.3% to $88.12.** Trump said the Hormuz energy shock cost Republicans the House. Translation: the White House now has a political reason to keep oil cheap. The war premium left the building. The **10-year yield fell 8 bps to 4.89%**, because nothing calms bond traders like a Congress that can't pass a spending bill.

Here is the part that doesn't fit. **Gold settled at $4,073 (+3.4%)**, then jumped another 1.6% overnight to **$4,137 in futures**. A relief rally is supposed to kill the safe haven. Gold didn't get the memo. The bond market says calm. Gold says it's not buying it. One of them is wrong, and gold has been right more often this year.

Overnight was a split screen. The **Hang Seng jumped 3.7%**. Nifty gained 2.4%. The **KOSPI fell 5.3%** and the Nikkei slid 1.8% as chipmakers got hit. **Nasdaq futures are down 0.5%** while S&P futures are up 0.15% at 7,732, because Qualcomm's weak forecast and Snap's revenue miss came with the morning coffee.

## What it means for you

Cheap oil is a gift to the people who burn it. **$88.12 WTI** helps consumer discretionary ([XLY](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XLY)) and transports ([XTN](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XTN)). It hurts upstream energy ([XLE](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XLE)), which was priced for a war that just got a ceasefire and a diplomat.

Lower yields and a cyclical rotation favor financials ([XLF](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XLF)) and industrials ([XLI](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XLI)) over long-duration tech. Qualcomm fell 7% on a memory shortage. The market is not rewarding anyone who says "supply chain" this week.

Gold is the tell. **Gold up 3.4% on a day stocks rallied 3%** means someone is buying protection while everyone else buys the dip. [GLD](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/GLD), [IAU](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/IAU) and the miners ([GDX](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/GDX)) deserve a fresh look.

Today's swing factor is the **Bank of England**. The expectation is a 25 bps hike to 4.00%, its first since August 2023. Bailey's press conference can move sterling, gilts and global duration. **Friday's NFP** remains the week's anchor. The **CME FedWatch odds of a December Fed hike sit at 82%**, so a hot print would make the relief rally look premature.

- Hold core gold ([GLD](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/GLD), [IAU](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/IAU)): futures at $4,137 pre-market, $4,000 now acting as support. Do not chase with fresh size, and do not sell into this trend.
