4/
The story nobody is covering: EU gas storage is at 74% against a historical 85 to 90% winter buffer. Oil is pricing peace. Europe is heading into winter short on gas. One of those prices is wrong.

So which market got it right? Equities say rotate. Bonds, gold and gas say inflation isn't done. One of them is wrong, and it's rarely the one with a 10-year yield attached.
