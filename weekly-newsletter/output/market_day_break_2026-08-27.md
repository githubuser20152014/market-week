## The Brief

Nvidia just printed **$96.2 billion** and guided to **$108B**, and futures know exactly what to do with that: Nasdaq futures up 1.1%, S&P up 0.48%. Bonds still refuse to rally with stocks, several sessions running now, and they are not being subtle about it.

Yesterday's drag was just pre-earnings jitters stacked on Jackson Hole dread. The **10Y creeping toward 4.70%** did the actual damage: higher long rates hate duration, and tech is nothing but duration, so allocators sold first and asked questions never. Then Nvidia showed up and answered anyway: **$96.2 billion in Q2 revenue**, up 106% year-over-year, a $108B Q3 guide, and Jensen Huang confirming Vera Rubin silicon is in full production. That is the entire gap between flat futures and Nasdaq futures up 1.1% overnight. Call it the [AI](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/AI) CapEx cycle producing receipts nobody asked for and everybody needed.
