Trump Said No Strike Today. Oil Fell 2.13%. The 10-Year Still Sits at 4.99%.

The Morning Brief · Market intelligence at the open

The President said "there will not be a strike today" on Iran. Oil heard it and dropped 2.13% to $101.07. That is the whole peace dividend: a hundred dollars a barrel and a good mood.

Friday was red and nobody sent a warning. The S&P 500 fell 0.52% to 7,623.68. The Dow dropped 1.23% to 52,215.16, which is a lot for the index that is supposed to be boring. Meta fell nearly 7% and Lam Research fell 9% on the AI capex hangover, and the money walked straight into defense and staples. Lockheed jumped 5%. P&G jumped 5%.

Futures are green anyway, up 0.3%, because nothing says confidence like buying ahead of the Fed.

Here's my read:

The 10-year yield is 4.99%. That is one tick from the 5% line that already broke equities once. The yield rose 3.5 bps Friday and the market is treating it like a rounding error. It is not a rounding error.

Gold closed at $4,032 and held $4,000 again, after Friday's margin-call flush. Sovereign and physical buyers treat that level like a floor. Gold is not trading like a bond proxy. It is trading as the hedge for geopolitics and fiscal chaos, and the market keeps supplying both.

Defense has actual backlog behind it: $292 billion at Lockheed, plus Houthi missiles getting intercepted over Riyadh. That is a fundamental, not a vibe. Europe is leading the open: DAX up 0.89%, CAC up 0.79%, FTSE up 0.44%, thanks to the Moody's France reprieve. Asia split on tech anxiety. Nikkei fell 0.80%, KOSPI dropped 1.46%, Hang Seng gained 0.51%.

The real events are the FOMC decision Wednesday (hold at 3.75%-4.00% expected) and Alphabet, Microsoft, and Amazon earnings. Today, watch whether the 10-year holds below 5.00%. A breach and hold above it turns green futures red fast.

The One Trade: LONG $GLD

Gold defended $4,000 twice in five trading days, with Asian physical buyers stepping in both times, while the 10-year sat at 4.99% and equities bounced. Buyers are not waiting for a rate cut to bid it.

Confirms: GLD holds above $388 (spot near $4,010) through 11 AM ET. If gold stays bid while S&P futures and Treasuries move in opposite directions, the floor thesis is real, not a bounce.

Kill switch: A clean break below $4,000 spot gold ($387 GLD) on a hawkish Wednesday Powell press conference with upward CPI dot projections. That kills the floor thesis and points to $3,950. Get out fast.
