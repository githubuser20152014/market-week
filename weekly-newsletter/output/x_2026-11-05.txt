1/ The Dow added 1,570 points. Democrats took Congress and Wall Street decided gridlock is a feature. Three markets disagree with the party.

2/ Bonds: 10-year yield down 8 bps to 4.89%. Crude: WTI down 8.3% to $88.12. Gold: up 3.4% to $4,073, then another 1.6% overnight. A relief rally that can't kill the safe haven.

3/ Qualcomm fell 7% on a weak forecast and a memory shortage hitting smartphone sales. Indexes were celebrating. A company with a front-row seat to the supply chain was not.

4/ The story nobody is covering: Korea. The KOSPI fell more than 5% overnight while Hong Kong rose 3.7% and India rose 2.4%. Same oil relief, very different winners. Chipmakers are paying for it.

5/ Equities say soft landing. Gold says don't be so sure. One of them is wrong, and gold has the better record this year.
