## The Brief

Stocks hit a record. The 10-year sits at **5.31%**. Brent is parked at $100 and the dollar is climbing. Everyone is calling this a Fed-pause rally, which is a generous name for ignoring three alarms at once.

The **Nasdaq closed at 27,477, a fresh all-time high** (+1.05%), and the S&P 500 added 0.66% to 7,773.95. The Dow managed +0.18%, because the rally is a mega-cap tech party and the rest of the index wasn't invited. **Brent held near $100** and WTI sat around $90, unimpressed by a G7 reserve release. Gold slipped 0.13% to $4,156.80, the dollar index rose 0.28% to 102.18, and the **10-year yield rose 3.4 basis points to 5.31%**. A firmer dollar, higher yields and stubborn oil add up to a valuation ceiling the equity market has decided not to look at.

Friday's **September payrolls came in at +29,000** against an 85,000 to 90,000 consensus. Fed-pause odds jumped above 78% for October 27-28, so bad news became good news again. Then [ISM](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ISM) Services Prices **spiked to 74.0**, the highest since July 2022. Labor is cooling and services inflation is not. That is one foot on the gas and one on the brake. The bond market noticed. Equities are only watching the gas pedal.

The story for ETF investors is **oil that will not leave $100**. The G7 and [IEA](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/IEA) released reserves, Middle East shipments are rising, and Brent still sits at $100 with Houthi attacks keeping traders on edge. That is not a market pricing relief. If crude stays here, [XLE](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XLE) and [MLPA](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MLPA) keep earning while the stagflation tax lands on airlines and industrials ([XTN](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XTN)). Tech's record run ([QQQ](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/QQQ), [XLK](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XLK)) works only while the Fed pause holds. A 5.31% yield is a rude backdrop for 30x earnings. **Bonds never bought this rally**, and [TLT](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/TLT) is a landmine: buy only if yields crack below 5.2%.

Futures point to a gap-up open: S&P at 7,860 (+0.44%), Nasdaq at 31,524 (+0.66%). Nikkei +1.05%, Hang Seng +1.00%, and KOSPI -0.89% for variety. Europe is 0.45% to 0.70% higher. **The swing factor is whether oil breaks out of $100 or finally cracks.** Either way, inflation expectations ([TIPS](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/TIPS) spreads) and the bond market move with it, and a bond reversal would drag rate-sensitive tech down too. No major U.S. data today, so the Paramount-Warner close and oil headlines run the tape. FOMC minutes Wednesday are the next scheduled event.
