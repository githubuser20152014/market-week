Micron Grew 11x. The 10-Year Hit 5.29% Anyway.

The Morning Brief · Market intelligence at the open

Happy new quarter. The 10-year Treasury yield just hit 5.29%, the highest since 2002. Futures are green anyway. Somebody forgot to tell the bond market it was supposed to join the party.

Yesterday was three indexes with three opinions. The Dow fell 0.86% to 50,906. The S&P 500 slipped 0.25% to 7,651. The Nasdaq rose 0.24% to 26,861. The Russell 2000 lost 0.39%, because small caps are the canaries of domestic credit, and these canaries have a cough.

Gold sat at $4,205. WTI climbed to $91.31 with Brent flirting with $100. Growth is slowing, inflation is not, and the Fed's October hike is still on the table. Slow car, hot engine, no brakes.

Here's my read:

Three things broke the tape. GDP was revised to 2.2% from 2.5%. August PCE printed +0.9% month over month. Chinese refiners reportedly banned October fuel exports, which sends oil higher and inflation right along with it.

Then there is the part nobody wants to say out loud. $40.1 trillion of US debt and $739 billion of Q3 issuance means the bond market wants paying. The 30-year sits at 5.67%. That is not a Fed story. That is a supply story, and supply does not care about Micron.

Speaking of Micron: data center revenue jumped 11-fold. Chips gap up at the open. One great earnings print is not a broad rally. Bonds never bought it, and the Dow just snapped a five-month winning streak.

Overnight, the Nikkei ripped 3.3% to 68,957 on semiconductor demand. The ASX 200 dropped 1.99%, Nifty fell 0.88%, and the FTSE 100 is down 1.12%. Rising US yields are a global tax, and Asia just got the bill. S&P futures are up 0.36% to 7,743. Nasdaq futures are up 0.77% to 30,934, which is the Nasdaq-100 contract, so ignore the gap to the Composite.

The swing factor today: Fed speakers, and whether the 10-year pushes through the 5.34% intraday high from earlier this week. A breach there ruins the afternoon, Micron or not.

The One Trade: LONG $SOXX

Micron's data center revenue jumped 11-fold and guidance beat across the board. The AI buildout is accelerating, not plateauing, and every major chip name in SOXX gets a re-rating today.

Confirms: SOXX holds above its prior day close within the first 45 minutes and Nasdaq futures stay above 30,700. The Nikkei's 3.3% surge on semiconductor demand is the international corroboration.

Kill switch: the 10-year breaks above 5.34% intraday and SOXX reverses below the open by 11:00 AM ET. That means the rate trade is overwhelming the earnings catalyst. Get out fast.

What else I'm watching

  • SMH: same read-through as SOXX. Add on confirmation, don't chase the gap. If NQ drops below 30,700, wait for a re-entry.
  • XLE / XOP: WTI at $91 with Chinese export bans and OPEC+ expected to roll quotas Sunday. Hold the bid while WTI stays above $89. Below that, the supply story loses to demand fears.
