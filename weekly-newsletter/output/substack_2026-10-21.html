KOSPI Jumped 3.29%. Homebuilders Hit a 2012 Low. Same Planet, Allegedly.

The Morning Brief · Market intelligence at the open

Oil fell 3.12% to $98.61 on a phone call. Brent was above $110 yesterday, a Persian Gulf tanker was on fire, and then Steve Witkoff flew to St. Petersburg and the entire risk premium evaporated.

The Dow added 530 points to close at 52,495. The Russell 2000 jumped 1.65%. The Nasdaq finished at 26,424, down 0.02%, because Netflix fell off a cliff after hours and tech decided to sit this one out.

Meanwhile the 10-year sits at 5.117% and homebuilder confidence just hit its lowest since 2012. One of these markets is celebrating. The other is holding a mortgage application and a lit match. Same planet, allegedly.

Here's my read:

The oil move is a diplomacy trade, and diplomacy trades have the shelf life of milk. A 60-day Russian diesel sanctions waiver landed, Hormuz fear got repriced in one session, and Republicans immediately revolted against the waiver. If Capitol Hill kills it, crude snaps back above $100. The peace dividend lasts exactly as long as the news cycle.

Now look at what 5% on the 10-year is doing to housing. Starts fell 5.2%. Builder confidence is at a 14-year low. Gold is sitting at $4,155 with real yields above 5%, which means central banks are buying it like it's going out of style. It's not a mood. It's structural.

Then there's Europe. The French 10-year hit 5% for the first time since 2002, and OAT-Bund spreads are widening into real stress. Check what your bond proxies actually hold.

Overnight, KOSPI ripped 3.29% on Samsung and SK Hynix. AI hardware demand is real, and apparently Seoul is the only place that got the memo.

The One Trade: LONG $ITA

Defense and aerospace is the only sector where earnings beats are actually being rewarded. Northrop Grumman and GE Aerospace both printed above consensus, SpaceX is up over 8% pre-market, and Witkoff-Putin diplomacy, even if it works, doesn't shrink a single Pentagon procurement budget. Defense doesn't need rate cuts or stable oil. It just needs the world to stay exactly as unhinged as it is.

Confirms: ITA holds above yesterday's close by 10:30 AM and SpaceX's gains spread into NOC and LMT opening green.

Kill switch: Congressional Republicans kill the Russian diesel waiver and crude spikes back above $103, rotating money out of defense and into energy. If that happens, get out fast.
