KOSPI Jumped 6.63%. A Missile Hit Oil's Peace Talks. Stocks Called It Optimism.

The Morning Brief · Market intelligence at the open

The Nasdaq ripped 1.18% to 26,477.81 on Tuesday while the 10-year sat at 4.993%, one rounding error shy of 5%. Stocks are celebrating at a yield that used to cause panic.

The S&P 500 added 0.60% to 7,722.60. The Russell 2000 jumped 1.16%. The Dow gained 0.03%, which is what a pulse looks like on a monitor. Gold slipped 0.25% to $4,064.60 and still refuses to leave $4,000. Oil closed at $101.68 (+1.78%), because someone has to be the adult in the room.

KOSPI exploded 6.63% to 6,012.44 after SK Hynix posted record quarterly earnings. Then a Houthi missile claim against Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery erased the oil diplomacy discount overnight. Chips up, crude up, yields up. Pick your favorite contradiction.

Here's my read:

Before 2:00 PM, this tape is pre-positioned and bored. The Fed holds at 3.75%-4.00%, a roughly 90% probability and already priced. What isn't priced is Powell on oil-driven inflation. If he leans hawkish, TLT gets hit again and growth multiples learn some humility.

After the close, Alphabet and Microsoft need to show cloud margin proof, not another capex slide. A Nasdaq at 26,478 into a near-5% yield is a bet that they will. Gold holding $4,000 against a 5% yield means sellers tried twice and failed.

Pre-market futures are mixed: S&P +0.08%, Nasdaq-100 +0.36%, Dow -0.25%. Europe is soft, with the FTSE 100 down 0.31% and sterling at 1.3283 after the UK Autumn Budget landed on gilts. The 2:00 PM FOMC statement is today's fulcrum: everything before it is noise, everything after it sets the risk for Big Tech.

The One Trade: LONG $XLE

A Houthi ballistic missile claim on Ras Tanura, the world's largest crude export terminal, reinstates a $10-$15 geopolitical risk premium that the Tehran diplomacy talks were briefly pricing out. Today's EIA inventory report is a second ignition switch for WTI already above $101.

Confirms: WTI holds above $100.50 through the 10:30 AM EIA report and XLE trades above its Tuesday close within the first 30 minutes.

Kill switch: EIA reports a crude build above 3 million barrels AND Saudi Aramco confirms no structural damage at Ras Tanura. That combination deflates the premium and breaks WTI toward $97. If it hits, get out fast.

What else I'm watching

  • Energy (XLE, XOP): don't you dare sell it into the 10:30 AM EIA report. Trim only on a surprise build above 3 million barrels.
  • Long-duration Treasuries (TLT, GOVT): stay away before 2:00 PM. If Powell pushes back on 2027 cuts, the 10-year breaks above 5.10% and TLT gets another leg down. Wait for the press conference.
