*Daily Edition · Market intelligence at the open*

## The Brief

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh hiked rates for the first time in three years, and equities responded by... rallying. Futures are ripping 1.5-2% higher this morning while bonds sit this one out entirely: the 10-year is glued near 5% and T-note futures barely budged. Gold and oil both sold off into the close and are flat pre-market, which means the inflation-hedge trade got benched the same week it was supposed to be right.

The S&P 500 closed at **7,551, down 0.45%**, the Dow shed 1.21%, and the Russell 2000 dropped 0.40%, all curtsying to Warsh's 25-basis-point hike to **3.75%-4.00%**, the first increase in three years and about as welcome as it sounds. The 10-year Treasury settled at **5.006%**, a level the market hasn't seen since 2007, back when "subprime" was still just a word and not a warning label. **Gold fell nearly 1% to $4,345** and WTI dropped 1.08% to $101.32, because Saudi Aramco quietly rerouted crude around the Strait of Hormuz mess via Oman's Sohar port, and the market's geopolitical risk premium evaporated on the spot.

Warsh's press conference was the real story. He told markets, in so many words, that services inflation and $40 trillion in sovereign debt supply mean rates stay restrictive until the data forces his hand, like a landlord raising rent the same week the tenant thought the lease was up. Oil's separate selloff just confirms the **Strait of Hormuz** premium is losing its grip on price, for now, because ship-to-ship transfers apparently solve geopolitics better than diplomacy does.

## What it means for you

The rate hike regime just reshuffled the entire allocation deck, and growth stocks lost their seat. **Leveraged growth and high-multiple tech face a structural headwind** because a 5% discount rate does not care about your 2028 earnings story. Short-duration Treasuries ([SHV](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SHV), [BIL](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/BIL)) and dividend aristocrats ([NOBL](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/NOBL), [VIG](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/VIG)) are the boring winners here, and boring is finally paying again. Energy ([XLE](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/XLE)) loses its fear premium while the Saudi bypass holds, but one bad headline out of the Houthi-Saudi standoff and that trade snaps back overnight. The real catalyst hiding in plain sight is the **September 24 Trump-Xi summit**: $30 billion in tariff relief on electronics and agriculture is the setup for tech ([QQQ](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/QQQ), [SOXX](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SOXX)) and ag exporters ([MOO](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MOO), [CORN](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/CORN)), assuming nobody tweets it into oblivion first.

Pre-market futures are pricing a gap-up open: **S&P futures at 7,682, up 1.67%**, Nasdaq futures up 2.06%, which is either short-covering relief or the market front-running a summit that hasn't happened yet. Europe is dead flat, offering exactly zero confirmation either way. Watch the 10-year: if it holds below 5.05% while stocks rally, fine, the story holds. If yields pop back above 5.05% while futures stay bid, that is not bullish divergence, that is the market lying to itself. And keep an eye on BOJ Governor Ueda: a confirmed hike to 1.25% in Tokyo sends yen volatility straight into every carry trade on the planet.
