Democrats Won Congress. Stocks Gained 0.36%. Bonds Said 4.97% Anyway.

The Morning Brief · Market intelligence at the open

Democrats took Congress overnight and futures promptly threw a fit. By morning they had crawled back to roughly +0.2%, because nothing says conviction like a round trip.

Yesterday the S&P 500 added 0.36% to 7,568 and the Nasdaq gained 0.83% to 25,372, led by semis after AMD posted data-center revenue that nearly doubled. The Dow shed 0.38% to 51,786. Apple's memory-chip margin hangover did not help.

One market refused to join the party. The 10-year yield climbed 5.6 basis points to 4.97% while stocks celebrated. The bond market watched the rally and politely declined.

Here's my read:

The catalyst is the midterms, not a data print. Divided government means gridlock, Wall Street loves gridlock, and that leaves the Fed as the only adult in the room. Bonds never bought the rally: the 10Y rose while equities recovered, which is a bond market rolling its eyes.

Crude is the other tell. WTI fell 0.9% to $95.21 on Qatar's draft US-Iran memorandum on Strait of Hormuz transit, deflating the risk premium that had crude above $104 a week ago. Peace is bearish for oil. Who knew. Gold slipped 0.7% to $3,940 and the dollar index dropped 0.43% to 101.43.

Into the open, S&P and Nasdaq futures are up about 0.2%, which reads as calm continuation, not gap-and-go. Europe is louder: DAX +1.5%, CAC +1.5%, Euro Stoxx +1.8%, all celebrating a US election they cannot vote in. The Nikkei fell 1.4% on a post-holiday chip sell-off, while the Kospi reversed a 4% intraday crash to close +0.6%. Someone is buying Korean memory on every dip.

The swing factor is the 10-year at 4.97%. If it breaks 5.00% on a data surprise, utilities, real estate and TLT get sold and growth multiples get squeezed.

The One Trade: LONG $SOXX

AMD's data-center revenue nearly doubling in one quarter confirms enterprise AI chip demand is structural, not cyclical. The Kospi reversing a 4% intraday crash to close green says institutional buyers are defending the semiconductor trade globally on every dip.

Confirms: SOXX holds above its prior-day close through the 10:30 AM ET check with broad chip names (AMD, NVDA, MU) all trading positive. If that holds, the institutional bid is real.

Kill switch: SOXX breaks below yesterday's low, or the 10-year prints above 5.00% intraday. Growth multiples get compressed and the dip-buying thesis dies. Get out fast.

What else I'm watching

  • Gold (GLD, PHYS): Down 0.7% to $3,940 is a retest, not a reversal. Don't you dare sell the core. Treat a dip toward $3,900 as a gift, and cut only if gold closes below $3,880 on a weekly basis.
  • Energy (XLE, XOP): With WTI at $95.21 the risk premium is leaving the building. Trim here and keep a 5% stub as a cheap hedge in case Qeshm Island diplomacy breaks down and crude snaps back above $100.
