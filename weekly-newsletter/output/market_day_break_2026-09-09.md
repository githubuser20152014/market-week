## The Brief

The dominant mood is stagflation shock: oil piercing $100/bbl on Houthi strikes against Saudi energy hubs and a Strait of Hormuz standstill is forcing institutional desks to reprice every rate-cut expectation built into equity multiples. Gold at $4,446 is bid, yields are creeping toward 4.81%, and the dollar is soft - that cross-asset combination reads as a geopolitical fear trade, not a growth trade. Futures are pointing down at the open with S&P futures off 0.38% and Dow futures losing another 280 points, confirming Tuesday's selloff has no floor and no seatbelt.

The S&P 500 closed at **7,673 (-0.58%)**, the Dow shed 628 points to **52,786 (-1.18%)**, and the Nasdaq dropped to **26,421 (-0.32%)**. The Russell 2000 fell **0.52%** to 2,960, confirming broad-based distribution rather than a tech-specific rotation. **Gold surged to $4,446 (+1.19%)**, WTI crude closed at **$94.89 (+2.00%)**, the 10-year yield ticked up 2.2 basis points to **4.806%**, and the dollar index dipped slightly to 98.79 - a soft dollar alongside rising yields and surging gold is a textbook geopolitical premium signal.

Two catalysts drove Tuesday's damage. First, coordinated Houthi drone and missile strikes against Saudi energy installations at Abha, Jazan, and Najran, combined with U.S. CENTCOM strikes disabling three Iranian tankers and Tehran's declaration of a maritime exclusion zone across the **Strait of Hormuz**, sent Brent crude above $100/bbl. Think of the Strait as a pipeline valve controlling 20% of global daily oil consumption - when that valve freezes, wholesale input costs spike instantly into every refinery, airline, and chemical manufacturer simultaneously. Second, Friday's nonfarm payrolls print of 162,000 already crushed rate-cut odds before the energy shock arrived. Now Treasury Secretary Bessent is running a debt buyback operation specifically to cap the 10-year near 4.80%, which is the only thing standing between orderly markets and a duration unwind - and it is held together with government-issued duct tape.
