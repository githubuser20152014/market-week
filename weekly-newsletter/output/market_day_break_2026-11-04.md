## The Brief

Democrats took Congress overnight and futures promptly threw a fit. By morning they had crawled back to roughly **+0.2%**, because nothing says conviction like a round trip. Meanwhile the **10Y sits at 4.97%** and the bond market is the only one in the room not pretending this is fine.

Yesterday the **S&P 500 added 0.36% to 7,568** and the **Nasdaq gained 0.83% to 25,372**, led by semis after [AMD](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/AMD)'s earnings pop and data-center revenue nearly doubling. The **Dow shed 0.38% to 51,786**, and Apple's memory-chip margin hangover did not help. The **10-year yield climbed 5.6 basis points to 4.97%**, so bonds watched the rally and politely declined. Gold slipped 0.7% to **$3,940**, WTI fell 0.9% to **$95.21**, and the **dollar index dropped 0.43% to 101.43**.

The catalyst is the midterms, not a data print. Divided government means gridlock, Wall Street loves gridlock, and that leaves the Fed as the only adult in the room. **Bonds never bought the rally**: the 10Y rose while equities recovered, which is a bond market rolling its eyes. WTI keeps sliding on Qatar's draft US-Iran memorandum on **Strait of Hormuz** transit, deflating the risk premium that had crude above $104 a week ago. Peace is bearish for oil. Who knew.
