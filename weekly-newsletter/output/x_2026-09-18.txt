1/ Nasdaq ripped 1.69% Friday on Nvidia's datacenter forecast. Small caps managed 0.55%. When one company does the heavy lifting for an index, that's concentration risk wearing a rally costume.

2/ Bonds and gold didn't buy the relief. The 10-year eased off 5.01% but still sits near 4.95%. Gold held $4,418 despite falling yields and a firmer dollar, the exact setup that's supposed to send it lower. It didn't budge.

3/ The proof point: Nvidia's datacenter outlook is basically the entire rally, not broad earnings strength. One company doing the work that used to take dozens of names.

4/ The angle nobody's covering: oil fell 5.79% after Saudi Aramco quietly rerouted tankers around Oman's Sohar terminal. Tanker logistics moved this market more than any Houthi headline did this week.

5/ So which market got it right Friday? Equities betting the hike shock is over, or bonds and gold still pricing a Fed that isn't done? One of them is wrong.
