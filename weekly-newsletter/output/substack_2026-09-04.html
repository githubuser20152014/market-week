Iran Mined the Strait of Hormuz. Oil Fell 0.81% Anyway.

The Morning Brief · Market intelligence at the open

Iran is laying naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz. The U.S. is prepping escort missions. And WTI crude closed at $90.56, down 0.81% on the day. Somewhere a risk desk decided a shooting war in the world's most important oil chokepoint is a rounding error.

Meanwhile Fed Governor Waller floated a September pause and the market threw itself a party. The S&P 500 ripped 1.06% to 7,747, the Nasdaq popped 1.40% to 26,584, the Dow tacked on 1.18% to 53,686. Gold didn't RSVP. It's still parked above $4,519 like it doesn't trust the invitation.

Futures are dead flat this morning, which means the 8:30 AM payrolls print gets to decide who was right.

Here's my read:

Waller cracked the door on a rate hold, and traders kicked it in like last call. The Russell 2000 limped in at just +0.51%, because small-caps only show up to the rally after the good seats are taken. Broadcom's earnings did the actual heavy lifting for tech: AI chip revenue up 221% to $16.7 billion, Q4 guidance of $21.7 billion, a rally that had nothing to do with rates for once. The 10-year yield eased to 4.762%, bonds shrugged, and Norway's sovereign wealth fund quietly announced it's trimming U.S. Treasury holdings, which is the kind of structural headwind that doesn't un-happen by lunch.

The One Trade: Long $GLD

Gold refused to sell into yesterday's equity rally and is holding $4,519 with geopolitical risk multiplying on three fronts: Strait of Hormuz mine deployment, German infrastructure sabotage attributed to Russia, and sovereign wealth fund diversification away from U.S. Treasuries.

Confirms: GLD holds above the $4,500 level by 10:00 AM ET regardless of the payrolls print direction. If equities rally on soft jobs data and GLD still doesn't sell, the institutional bid is confirmed.

Kill switch: Payrolls print well above consensus (above 100K), yields spike sharply, the dollar index breaks above 100, and GLD breaks below $4,480 on volume, get out fast.
