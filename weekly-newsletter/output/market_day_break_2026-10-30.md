AMD, Broadcom, and Nvidia fell in sympathy. Investors used to fund construction on the promise of future rent. Now they want cash at the door.

Overnight, the **Bank of Japan held at 1.25%** and declared its 2% inflation target sustainably reached. It also sped up its ETF sales. Nikkei down 2.33%, Kospi through the circuit breakers. The **Strait of Hormuz** is still the neighborhood fire: Iran claims four tankers struck mines, and WTI sits above $104.
