Wednesday Cheered Gridlock. Thursday the Dow Lost 1,833 Points. Gridlock Was a One-Day Rental.

The Morning Brief · Market intelligence at the open

Wednesday, the market cheered a Democratic sweep for delivering gridlock. Thursday, it read the fine print.

The S&P 500 dropped 4.4% to 7,353. The Nasdaq plunged 5.5% to 24,315, a correction in one session. The Dow shed 1,833 points to 51,523. Nvidia fell roughly 9%. AMD and Micron dropped over 17%. The VIX spiked to 28.18.

That is not a macro scare. That is the most crowded trade on Earth discovering the exit is a door, singular. Gridlock was apparently a one-day rental.

Here's my read:

The catalyst was explicit. A newly empowered Democratic House and Senate, plus local ballot measures rejecting data-center zoning, put hyperscaler power consumption in the regulatory crosshairs. AI infrastructure got repriced from "inevitable" to "permit pending."

Then the bonds did the funniest thing. The 10-year yield fell to 4.837% as capital fled equities. Gold closed at $4,077, down just 0.88% on a day the Nasdaq lost 5.5%. Gold sat out the panic like it had a reservation somewhere nicer.

WTI held above $90.88. The dollar firmed to 101.96. The Nikkei fell 5.96% and KOSPI fell 6.02%, so the contagion was a group project.

Futures say relief: S&P up 0.36%, Nasdaq up 1.7%. Dow futures say no, down 0.9%. That divergence is a tech-only bounce, not a change of heart. Then the 8:30 AM October NFP print decides everything. Above 200K with hot wages revives the December Fed hike and hits tech multiples again. Below 150K hands the Fed an excuse to pause. Watch Nasdaq 24,000 as the line in the sand.

The One Trade: LONG $GLD

Gold lost less than 1% on a day the Nasdaq crashed 5.5% and the VIX hit 28.18. That relative strength confirms $4,000/oz as institutional support, and the NFP print creates a binary entry window with asymmetric upside on any soft number.

Confirms: GLD holds above $370 (approximate spot equivalent) through the first 30 minutes post-NFP at 9:00 AM, with the DXY failing to sustain a move above 102.5.

Kill switch: October NFP prints above 220K with average hourly earnings above 0.4% month-over-month, DXY surges past 103, and GLD breaks below $368 on volume. That invalidates the thesis and means the margin-call cash-raising is not done. Get out fast.

  • Gold (GLD/IAU): don't you dare sell the core above $4,000/oz. A soft NFP (sub-150K) and a weaker dollar means add on dips toward $4,020-4,050. Hot NFP and DXY above 103, trim tactically, never exit.
  • Semis (SMH): stay away through the open. Nvidia -9%, AMD -17%, Micron -17%. This is a regulatory re-rating, not a one-day flush. Wait for the VIX to close below 22.
