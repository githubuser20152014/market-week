Wednesday, the market cheered a Democratic sweep for delivering gridlock.

Thursday, it read the fine print. The S&P 500 fell 4.4%. The Nasdaq fell 5.5% into a correction in a single session. Here's why you shouldn't trust the clean story that gridlock was good news.

The crowd that sold didn't sell "the economy." It sold one trade: AI infrastructure. A newly empowered Congress plus local ballot measures rejecting data-center zoning put hyperscaler power consumption in the regulatory crosshairs. Nvidia dropped roughly 9%. AMD and Micron dropped over 17%. The VIX spiked to 28.18. The most crowded trade on Earth just discovered the exit is a door, singular.

Now look at the markets that refused to panic.

Bonds: the 10-year yield fell to 4.837% as capital fled equities. That is the old flight-to-safety playbook working exactly as written, which tells you this was fear, not a growth scare.

Gold: closed at $4,077, down only 0.88% on a day the Nasdaq lost 5.5%. Gold sat out the panic like it had a reservation somewhere nicer. When the safe haven shrugs at a crash, the market is telling you the damage is concentrated, not systemic.

Crude: WTI held above $90. Nobody sold the real economy. They sold the story about the future economy.

The proof point: Nikkei fell 5.96% and KOSPI fell 6.02% overnight. The chip supply chain is global, so the unwind was too. Contagion was a group project.

The second-order story nobody is covering: power. If data-center zoning becomes a ballot-box issue, the bottleneck for AI is not chips or capital. It is a county commission vote. Utilities, grid equipment and local politics just became part of the AI thesis.

So which market got it right? Equities said the buildout is in trouble. Bonds said we are scared. Gold said we are fine. Futures this morning say relief, but Dow futures are down 0.9% while Nasdaq futures are up 1.7%, which is a tech-only bounce, not a change of heart. The 8:30 AM jobs report decides which story survives the open.

My read: the regulatory repricing is real, and one green futures screen does not undo it.
