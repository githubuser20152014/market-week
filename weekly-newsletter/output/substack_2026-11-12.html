Nvidia Rallied 2.87%. The Board Needed One Dinner. $300 Billion Gone.

The Morning Brief · Market intelligence at the open

Wednesday was a victory lap. The S&P 500 jumped 1.31% to 7,619.38. Semis ripped 5.7%. Nvidia closed up 2.87% at $173.73, and every AI bull in America went to dinner feeling like a genius.

Then the Wall Street Journal dropped the dessert. Nvidia's board is reportedly preparing to oust Jensen Huang over two alleged affairs, one with a suspected Chinese spy. The stock fell 7% to 9% after hours. Roughly $300 billion of market cap disappeared between the entree and the check.

Nvidia has confirmed nothing. No 8-K. No statement. Just a board meeting that reportedly ran into the night. The most valuable AI company on Earth is now a soap opera with a ticker.

Here's my read:

This is a governance story, not a demand story. Nobody cancelled a single GPU order overnight. They cancelled a CEO, maybe. Futures know it: Nasdaq -0.77%, S&P -0.37%, Dow +0.06%. The damage is one stock deep and one index wide.

Meanwhile the macro tape is doing its own thing. Gold lost 1.1% to $3,987.93 on a hot CPI print and a stronger dollar, and futures are already at $3,953. The 10-year sits at 4.99%, and that is Tuesday's close, because the bond market took Veterans Day off. Nobody told the Fed hike bets. Waller is floating a 50 bp December hike. Crude closed at $86.53, up 0.57%, and the old rule that falling oil drags yields down is dead and buried.

Asia took the first punch: Nikkei -0.70%, Hang Seng -1.26%. Europe is up 0.7% to 0.9% like it slept through the whole thing. DAX +0.86%, CAC +0.71%, and the UK posted a +0.7% Q3 GDP beat. Zero Nvidia in those indexes. Sometimes the best trade is not owning the problem.

The One Trade: LONG $SMH

The semis index ripped 5.7% on Wednesday, and the one headline that could undo it is about a man, not a chip. SMH still carries Nvidia as a top weight, so call it a cushion, not a force field.

Confirms: Nvidia's board backs Huang or names an interim CEO before 9:30 AM ET, or SMH holds most of Wednesday's semis gain through the first 30 minutes of trading.

Kill switch: Nvidia opens down more than 10% with no board statement and SMH hands back the whole Wednesday pop. Get out fast.

What else I'm watching

  • Nvidia statement or 8-K before 9:30 AM. A backing statement means buy the dip in SMH, not single-stock NVDA. Silence means trim the single-stock concentration and hold SMH as the cleaner AI proxy.
  • TLT: stay away. 4.99% on the 10-year, a hot October CPI, and a December hike priced in. Park dry powder in SGOV or BIL and get paid to wait.
  • Gold below $4,000: don't you dare short it. A stronger dollar and Fed hike bets did this, not a collapse in bullion demand. It is 24% under the January record of $5,318, which makes GLD an accumulation window for patient hands.
  • 8:30 AM jobless claims: soft print, Treasuries get a bid and tech gets a floor. Hot print, the 10-year goes through 5% and the tech gap-down deepens. Stay flat QQQ and add to SGOV.
  • Europe via EZU or FEZ: the trade nobody is talking about. No Nvidia in the index, a UK GDP beat, and a relative-value setup. The risk is an ECB hike narrative that spikes Bund yields.
