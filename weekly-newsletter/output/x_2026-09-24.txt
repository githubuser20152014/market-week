1/ The economy just printed its fastest growth in over five years. The S&P fell 0.75% anyway.

Good news is now bad news. The bond market is holding the pen, and the 10-year just hit 5.11%, a 19-year high.

2/ While equities sold off, three other markets each told their own story.

Bonds: yield up nearly 15 bps in a day.
Gold: down 1.33% as the dollar rallied.
Crude: up 1.8% on Hormuz risk.

Three markets. Three different panic attacks.

3/ Proof it wasn't just a screen glitch: Newmont fell 4.1% yesterday, more than the S&P, as metals slid and the dollar firmed.

Even the safe-haven trade couldn't dodge the math.

4/ The second-order story: oil is feeding the Fed.

Energy prices push inflation. Inflation pushes hike odds (roughly 77% for October). A possible US diesel export ban has big business writing angry letters.

Crude is the input nobody priced.

5/ Stocks say growth is fine. Bonds say the Fed is not done.

One of them is wrong, and the 10-year has the better track record.
